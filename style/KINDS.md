# Kinds of video

Roma Tom started as pure advice: one problem, one fix. Advice still matters most, but a feed made
only of lessons reads like homework, and a lot of what owners watch, save and share isn't a
lesson at all. Mix the kinds: about half advice, never two of the same kind in a row.

## What's working beyond advice (researched November 2026)

Day-in-the-life is the biggest format around owners by far. Relatable posts and stories get the
most **shares** compared with their views, which is how a new account gets found: an owner
sends it to their staff group chat with "this is us".

## The kinds

Every kind still has a hook (`style/HOOKS.md`), stays kind, and stays honest: characters' stories
are clearly the cast's (Roma Tom, Maria, Dev, Ivan, Sam), and numbers still need fact notes.

| Kind | What it is | How it ends | Templates that suit it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `advice` | one problem, one fix (the original) | the `fix` scene | any |
| `day` | a day, morning or closing at Roma Tom's cafe, or a regular's morning, told in times | a small warm moment, not a lesson | `day`, `rush`, `chat`, `tickets` |
| `relatable` | a moment every owner knows, played for a smile | a punchline, or "Tag the owner who…" said kindly | `chat`, `quote`, `floor`, `rush`, `countdown` |
| `guests` | the people who come in: types, habits, what they notice | a smile or one light observation | `countdown`, `quote`, `regulars` |
| `story` | something that happened to the cast, beginning to end | what changed; a lesson only if it falls out of the story | `day`, `chat`, `quote`, `review` |
| `hot take` | an opinion stated plainly, with the reason | the opinion again, and "Agree?" for the comments | `myth`, `scale`, `quote` |
| `quiz` | a guess first, the answer at the end | the answer, then "What did you guess?" | `receipt`, `jar`, `versus`, `menu`, `floor` |
| `then and now` | year one versus year ten, opening day versus today | the one thing that changed most | `versus`, `countdown` |
| `shout-out` | appreciation for owners, staff or a role (the dishwasher, the opener) | a kind word; something people send to their team | `quote`, `day`, `countdown` |

**Writing the non-advice kinds**

- Same length, same voice, same rules: about 25 to 40 seconds, American English, numbers
  spelled out in the voice, no emoji.
- No `fix` scene needed. End on the punchline, the answer, the feeling or a question for the
  comments, then the end card.
- The end card can change its line with `cta`: "Follow for more *cafe life*" suits day, story
  and relatable videos; keep "Follow for more *owner* tips" for advice.
- The caption follows the kind: a story caption tells the story; a relatable one can be a single
  line and a question ("Which one came in today?"). Still 3 to 5 hashtags, still no emoji.
- Recurring cast makes stories pay off: Maria the regular, Dev the line cook, Ivan the reviewer,
  Sam the supplier. Keep them consistent from video to video.
