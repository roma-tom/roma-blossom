## Fact notes

- **Example figures** (said as "Say…"): an extra ounce of fries at 10¢. Frozen fries wholesale
  run roughly $1.20 to $1.60 a pound, about 8¢ to 10¢ an ounce, so 10¢ is a round, slightly
  high example. 150 plates a day and 360 open days are an example cafe.
- **Worked calculation:** $0.10 × 150 × 360 = $5,400 a year. The hook's number is this one.
- The fix is standard portion control practice (portion scoops, spot-check weights, a plate
  photo at the pass), not a statistic.

## Post caption

A slightly bigger scoop on every plate feels generous. One extra ounce of fries at 10 cents, on 150 plates a day, 360 days a year, is $5,400. Give the line the right size scoop, weigh one plate every shift, and pin up a photo of how the plate should look.
