Free refills. Free for who? Say one refill pours fifty cents of coffee plus ten cents of milk and sugar. That's 60 cents for zero dollars, and at 25 refills a day it comes to about 5,400 dollars a year. Refills still bring guests back, so keep them, just on purpose: one free refill for dine-in, or a dollar a refill, and know what each cup costs you.
