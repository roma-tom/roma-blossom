What does it really cost when one barista quits? Add up the job ad, the interviews, a week of training pay, your trainer's time, the remakes and a slower line, and it comes to about 2,000 dollars. Lose three in a year and that's 6,000 dollars. A dollar an hour raise runs about 1,300 dollars a year, so ask your team what would keep them, and train every new hire properly.
