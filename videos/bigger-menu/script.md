## Fact notes

- **Example figures** (said as "Say…"): weekly sales per dish (40, 35, 2, 2) and $15 a week of
  spoiled ingredients for each slow dish that uses ingredients nothing else on the menu uses.
- **Worked calculation:** $15 × 5 × 52 = $3,900 a year.
- Deliberately not used: the "jam study" (24 jams versus 6) often quoted for choice overload.
  Later replications and a meta-analysis found little or no effect, so the video argues from
  waste instead of from psychology.

## Post caption

More dishes feels like more sales, but slow dishes need their own ingredients, and those spoil. Throw out $15 a week on each of five slow dishes and that's $3,900 a year. Cut what sells least, build dishes from shared ingredients, and bring the fun ones back as specials.
