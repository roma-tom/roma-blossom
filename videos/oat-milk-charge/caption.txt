Should you charge extra for oat milk? Oat milk can cost you about a dollar per latte, against a quarter for dairy. Give that 75 cents away on thirty drinks a day and it adds up to around 8,100 dollars a year. Charge about the real cost gap, show it clearly on the menu board, or add a little to every latte so nobody gets a surprise at the register.
