# oat-milk-charge

**Question:** "Should you charge extra for oat milk?"
**Background:** sky (pad: mellow)
**Length:** about 33 s

The idea: oat milk costs you several times more than dairy per drink. Giving it away at the
dairy price quietly adds up over a year. Charge about the real cost gap, openly, or spread it
across every latte so nobody gets a surprise at the till.

## Fact notes

All money figures are **example numbers for a typical independent cafe**, framed on screen and
in the voice with "Say…". Swap-in costs vary by supplier and region.

- **Milk per latte:** about 8 oz of milk for a 12 oz latte, allowing for some steaming waste
  (example).
- **Dairy, 25c:** whole milk at about $4.00 a gallon (128 oz) = 3.1c per oz; × 8 oz = **$0.25**.
- **Oat, $1.00:** a barista oat milk at about $4.00 per 32 oz carton = 12.5c per oz; × 8 oz =
  **$1.00**.
- **Gap:** 1.00 − 0.25 = **75c** per drink.
- **Year:** 0.75 × 30 oat drinks a day × 360 open days = **$8,100**. Voice rounds to "about eight
  thousand dollars".
- **Maria's quote:** a character's view, not a statistic.
- **Fix:** price the add-on at about the cost difference rather than as a penalty; list it
  clearly; or, as some cafes do, drop the surcharge and raise all milk drinks slightly so plant
  milks cost the same.
