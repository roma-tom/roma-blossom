# staff-come-first

**Topic:** The customer isn't always king. Your staff are.
**Kind:** hot take (see `style/KINDS.md`): the opinion, the reason, then "Agree?" for the comments
**Hook type:** hook-rule (note "The customer is always right", stamped NOT ALWAYS)
**Background:** lime (pad: sway)
**Voice:** speech, Roma Tom narrates; Dev says his own line
**Length:** about 31 s

The idea: "the customer is always right" can cost you the people who make the guest experience.
Backing your team against a rude guest is worth more than that one visit. Stated as an opinion,
not a statistic.

## Fact notes

An opinion piece with no statistics shown or spoken.

- **Scale weights (1 vs 3):** illustrative drawing of the opinion, not data.
- **"Hundreds of good guests":** an example of scale, not a figure: a barista serving even a
  few dozen guests a shift serves hundreds in a few weeks (for example 60 guests a shift × 5
  shifts = 300 a week).
- **Dev's line:** the cast's story (Dev, the recurring line cook), not a claim about staff in
  general.
- The cost of losing a barista was covered with worked numbers in `videos/replace-a-barista`.
