## Fact notes

- **"Twenty minutes", "Drove 20 minutes":** Ivan's story, a made-up guest; not a statistic.
- **$2,340 a year (scene 4):** example figures for a made-up cafe. 3 guests a week who find
  wrong hours and go elsewhere × $15 average check × 52 weeks = $2,340. Said as "over two
  thousand dollars". The line frames it as an example ("Say three guests a week…").
- **70% more likely to visit (scene 5):** Google, "Tips to improve your local ranking" and
  Business Profile help, citing Ipsos research: customers are 70% more likely to visit, and
  2.7 times more likely to consider reputable, a business with a complete Business Profile.
  https://support.google.com/business/answer/10515606 (also summarized at
  https://uberall.com/en-us/resources/blog/how-to-complete-your-google-business-profile-to-boost-local-seo-traffic).
- **"Google only shows what you tell it":** Google's own advice is to keep hours, special
  (holiday) hours and info up to date: https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091

## Post caption

Your Google page says open. Your door says closed. Guess which one the guest believes. Wrong hours send people to a locked door, and some of them leave a review on the way home. Once a month, check your hours, add holiday hours early and keep your menu and photos current. It only takes a few minutes.
