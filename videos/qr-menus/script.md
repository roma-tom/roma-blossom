# qr-menus

**Question:** "Do guests actually like QR code menus?"
**Background:** butter (pad: sunny)

The idea: QR menus are cheap to update, but some guests find them a hurdle, especially with weak
Wi-Fi. Keep paper available, test the code, and let servers offer both.
