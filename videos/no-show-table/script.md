## Fact notes

All figures are **example numbers for a typical cafe**, not real data; the caption says so.
- $25 x 4 guests = $100 spend. Ingredients 30% of sales = $30 (a common food cost target). 100 - 30 = **$70**.
- 70 x 2 x 52 = **$7,280**. Ingredients counted as saved only if not yet prepped; labour and rent still run regardless.
- Fixes are general practice (reminders, card holds, waitlists); no statistics claimed.
