## Fact notes

All figures are examples for a typical independent restaurant, not real data.

- Table of four, $100 booked, food cost 30% (common target) = $30 not spent. Loss = 100 - 30 = **$70** (ingredients only; labour and rent are paid either way).
- 70 x 2 x 52 = **$7,280** a year.
- Fix steps: general practice (reminder texts, card holds on larger parties, waitlists); no statistic claimed.
