# marias-hundredth-visit

**Topic:** Maria's first visit, and her hundredth
**Kind:** story (see `style/KINDS.md`): something that happened to the cast, beginning to end
**Hook:** custom (`hook.js`): Maria at an empty counter on her first visit, tapping the bell while the wall clock spins; nobody comes and she turns toward the door
**Background:** sky (pad: drift)
**Voice:** speech; Maria says the hook and her quote, Roma Tom narrates the rest
**Length:** about 33 s

The story: a year ago Maria nearly walked out because nobody greeted her. Dev's "Morning!"
kept her. Today is her hundredth visit. The lesson falls out of the story: every regular starts
as a first visit.
