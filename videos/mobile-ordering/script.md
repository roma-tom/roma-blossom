# mobile-ordering

**Question:** "Why do my guests love ordering on their phone?"
**Background:** paper
**Length:** 22.4 s

The idea: guests don't love the app, they love what it saves them: the line, the pressure at the
till, and remembering their usual. If you offer mobile ordering, make those three things easy.

## Fact notes

This video uses **no statistics**. Everything shown is an illustration, not data from a real business.

- **Rush bars** (guests per hour, 7 am to noon: 18, 34, 26, 15, 12, 22) are an example shape of a
  typical cafe morning, labelled "EXAMPLE" on screen. The peak number (34) is part of the example.
- **Menu prices** ($4.80, $4.50) are example cafe prices; the notes describe common mobile-ordering
  features (custom modifiers, photos and allergen info, saved/repeat orders).
- **The review** is a made-up example from our recurring character Sam, not a real review.
- **The reasons** (skipping the queue, customising without pressure at the till, one-tap reorders)
  are the commonly cited benefits of order-ahead apps; they're framed as reasons, not percentages.
- The fix steps are general practice: a short, clear digital menu; an honest ready time (a late
  "ready in 5 min" undoes the whole benefit); and saved favourites for regulars.
