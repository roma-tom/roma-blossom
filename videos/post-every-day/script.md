## Fact notes

- **"A couple of feed posts a week":** Adam Mosseri, head of Instagram, answering "how often
  should I post?" on Instagram's @creators account in 2021: "A couple of feeds a week, a couple
  of Stories per day." Summarized at https://www.shopify.com/blog/how-often-post-instagram and
  https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/how-often-to-post-on-instagram. Said as "once suggested",
  since it is a 2021 starting point, not a current rule.
- **"Over a hundred thousand accounts… about five times the engagement per post":** Buffer's
  consistency study of 100,000+ users: accounts that posted in 20 or more of 26 weeks earned
  about 5x more engagement per post than sporadic posters.
  https://buffer.com/resources/consistent-posting-study . The study is correlational and
  measures engagement, not reach; the line says "engagement per post".
- **Honest caveat (not on screen):** Buffer's separate 2-million-post analysis finds more posts
  a week do bring more total reach (3 to 5 a week as a sweet spot,
  https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/). So the take is "steady beats
  daily-then-burnt-out", not "posting less is better". Three a week sits in that range.
- **The calendar:** November 2026 starts on a Sunday; the ticks fall on Tuesdays, Thursdays and
  Saturdays.

## Post caption

Post every day or disappear, they say. That rule burns owners out. Even the head of Instagram once suggested a couple of feed posts a week. What matters is showing up every week. Three posts a week you can actually keep beats seven you quit after a month. Agree?
