# lattes-to-pay-rent

**Topic:** Guess how many lattes it takes to pay the rent
**Kind:** quiz (see `style/KINDS.md`): a guess first, the answer at the end, then "What did you guess?"
**Hook:** custom (`hook.js`): the month's rent bill unrolls down the screen while to-go lattes stack up beside it; the stack stops well short and a question mark lands on top
**Background:** paper (pad: sunny)
**Voice:** speech, Roma Tom narrates
**Length:** about 34 s

The idea: rent feels like one number on the first of the month. Counted in lattes, after the
cost of making each one, it's a thousand cups, or about thirty-three a day before anything else
gets paid.

## Fact notes

All money figures are **example numbers for a small independent cafe**, framed with "Say…".

- **Latte, $5.50:** a typical 12 oz latte price at an independent US cafe (example).
- **Espresso, 50¢:** a double shot of about 18 g at about $12 a pound (2.6¢ a gram) ≈ 48¢.
- **Milk, 30¢:** about 10 oz of whole milk at about $4 a gallon (3.1¢ an oz) ≈ 31¢.
- **Cup and lid, 20¢:** a 12 oz hot cup, lid and sleeve (example).
- **Kept per latte:** 5.50 − 0.50 − 0.30 − 0.20 = **$4.50** (before labor, rent and other costs).
- **Rent, $4,500 a month:** example rent for a small cafe space.
- **Answer:** 4,500 ÷ 4.50 = **1,000 lattes a month**.
- **Per day:** 1,000 ÷ 30 = 33.3, said as **thirty-three a day**.
