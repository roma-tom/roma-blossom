## Fact notes

All figures are **example numbers for a typical cafe**, not real data (said in the caption).

- Check: 4 guests x $25 = $100. Food and drink cost at 30% of the check = $30. Lost: 100 - 30 = **$70** (labour and rent are paid either way).
- Jar: 70 x 2 no-shows a week x 52 weeks = **$7,280**.
- Fixes are general practice: reminders, card holds and waitlists are common ways to cut no-shows.
