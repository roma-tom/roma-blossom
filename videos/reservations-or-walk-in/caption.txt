Should you take reservations, or stay walk-in only? Reservations help you plan staff and prep for the night. Walk-in only keeps things simple, with no no-shows to chase. Most places do best with both: take bookings for big tables and busy nights, and keep some tables open for walk-ins. Running a quick cafe? Walk-in is usually all you need.
