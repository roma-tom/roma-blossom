# staff-quit

**Question:** "Why do good staff quit after three months?"
**Background:** blush · **Music:** dreamy · **Cover:** crowd (Dev, Maria, Sam)

## Scenes
1 hook: STAFF / Why do good staff quit after *three months*?
2 myth: "They left for a bit more pay." / Often it's a rough first month: no plan, no feedback, no name to ask.
3 countdown: A written first-week plan / One buddy to ask anything / A real chat at week four
4 fix: Write down day one to five / Name a buddy for each new hire / Book the week-four chat now
5 endcard

## Fact notes
No statistics are used. This is practical advice from common onboarding practice; the myth card says "often", not a percentage. Pay matters too; the video does not claim otherwise.

## Post caption
Good people rarely leave out of nowhere. A clear first week, a buddy and a week-four chat go a long way. 🍅
