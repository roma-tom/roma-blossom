Happy hour fills the room, but does it fill the register? Say a glass of house wine is 9 dollars and 5 at happy hour. After the wine, the labor and the card fee, the happy hour glass keeps about 1.35 dollars against 5.23 at full price. That's almost four happy hour glasses to match one. Run it only in hours that would sit empty, pair the deal with food, and compare sales to a normal week.
