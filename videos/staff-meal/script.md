## Fact notes

- **Example figures** (said as "say it's…"): a $14 rice bowl whose ingredients cost $3.50,
  a 25% food cost, inside the 25% to 35% range commonly used as a target for restaurants.
- The countdown and the fix are common kitchen practice (a family meal before service,
  tasting new dishes with the team), not statistics.
- Not covered on screen: tax and wage rules for employee meals vary by state; the video makes
  no tax claim.

## Post caption

If leftovers are the only free food your staff get, look at what that tells them. A real staff meal costs you food cost, not menu price: a $14 rice bowl might be $3.50. For that, nobody works the rush hungry, your team can sell dishes they've tasted, and they feel looked after.
